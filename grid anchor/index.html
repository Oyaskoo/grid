Essential Business Features

Basic Pages

The foundation of your online presence. We build the right type of website to match your business goals and budget.

  • Landing page (single page, focused and conversion-driven)
  • Multi-page website (Home, About, Services, Contact)
  • Portfolio website (showcase your work professionally)
  • Simple product showcase page (display products with pricing)

Conversion Features

Turn website visitors into real enquiries and customers. These features make it easy for people to contact you the moment they are ready.

  • WhatsApp chat button (floating, always visible)
  • Click-to-call button (one tap to call on mobile)
  • Contact form (connected to Google Forms or email)
  • Call-to-action buttons (placed strategically)

Interact with conversion widgets on a mobile prototype.

Design Features

First impressions matter. We design websites that are visually refined, modern, and crafted to reflect your brand with precision.

  • Modern UI templates (clean, professional, industry-appropriate)
  • Basic animations (fade-in on scroll, smooth transitions)
  • Image sliders and carousels (showcase images cleanly)
  • Hero section with image or video background

Test real-time animations, transitions, and slider controls.

Local Business Features

Built for businesses that serve a local community. These features help nearby customers find you, trust you, and choose you.

  • Google Maps embed (your location on every website)
  • Business hours section (clearly displayed, easy to update)
  • Testimonials section (showcase what real clients say)
  • FAQ section (answer common questions, reduce friction)

Preview mock locations, schedules, reviews, and mini FAQ toggles.

Growth Features

Your website should grow with your business. These features ensure you are discoverable on Google and connected across all platforms.

  • Basic SEO setup (page title, meta description, keywords)
  • Google Analytics integration (track who visits your site)
  • Social media links integration (Instagram, Facebook, etc.)
  • Simple blog setup (publish articles to rank on Google)

See Google search snippet mockups and analytics trackers.

Automation Features

Save hours every week by letting your website do the work. These automations handle enquiries, notifications, and follow-ups for you.

  • Form to Google Sheets integration (saved automatically)
  • Email notifications for every new enquiry (instant alerts)
  • WhatsApp redirect on form submission (direct enquiry)
  • Newsletter signup connected to Mailchimp (free plan)

Simulate an enquiry pipeline flowing to Sheets, Mail & WhatsApp.

Business Add-ons

Take your website a step further with features that handle bookings, payments, and document sharing — all without a backend.

  • Basic booking system via Calendly (free plan integration)
  • Payment links via Razorpay or Stripe (accept payments directly)
  • Downloadable files (menu PDF, brochure, price list)

Try the mockup schedulers, Razorpay checkouts, and file download triggers.
